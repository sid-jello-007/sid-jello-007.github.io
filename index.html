How I work

Four rules I keep coming back to

Problem first, model second

Start from the handoff that hurts, not the technology.

Evals before agents

Agree what good looks like, then build to it.

Guardrails are features

Show users and risk partners when they kick in, and why.

Measure flow, not activity
